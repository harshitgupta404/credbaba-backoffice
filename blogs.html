Google Sheets Multi-Device Setup Guide

To allow blogs and users to sync seamlessly across different computers, phones, and team members without 403 Forbidden errors, ensure your Google Apps Script Web App has public permissions.

How to enable multi-device sync in 1 minute:
  1. Open your Google Sheet (e.g. CredBaba Blogs).
  2. Click Extensions > Apps Script from the top menu.
  3. Make sure the latest code from apps-script/blog-script.gs is pasted and saved.
  4. Click Deploy (top right) → Manage deployments.
  5. Click the ✏️ Edit icon next to the active Web App.
  6. Set Version: New version.
  7. Set Who has access: Anyone (Not "Only myself").
  8. Click Deploy. Copy the URL and verify it in Backoffice Settings.
💡 Instant Cross-Device Workaround: If you are switching devices right now, click "Export Blogs" on your previous device, and then "Import Blogs" here to instantly transfer all articles without waiting!
